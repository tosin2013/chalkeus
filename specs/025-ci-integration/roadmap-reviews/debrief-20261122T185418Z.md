- the fixture run (to the end, past its first PR);

- `step --once` against test targets (`TestStepMode`, `TestStepGaps`).

It asserts isolation checks (P01–P10, GM01–GM04, A01–A05), evidence and audit rows, against a
fake GitHub in this repository with no secrets. It passed on GitHub-hosted runners on #140, #146
and #147, in under 20 minutes. SC-001 holds for five of the six live bugs: each, put back, failed
its scenario (evidence/README.md). The glue packages have a unit-coverage floor, and the
scenarios' coverage of them is measured (33–79% vs 15–23% unit).

The updates are two scope items the spec didn't carry over from the roadmap, and the "only
rises" wording of the floor. No constraint is violated.
